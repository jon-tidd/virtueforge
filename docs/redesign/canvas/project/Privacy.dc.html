Privacy, in plain words

What we keep, and what we never do.

First names and drawings only. No photos, no face scans, no birthdays. You draw each child; we never see what they look like.
Kids never type or chat. Grown-ups add everything, and you approve anything personal before it's read aloud.
How stories are made. Each season is drafted with AI and reviewed by a person. Nightly details, like names and deeds, are woven in by AI, checked automatically, and shown to you first.
Voices stay in the family. A story voice is made only by the grown-up it belongs to, after they read a consent line aloud (kept, dated), and never from a child. It reads only our chapters, can't be downloaded, and they can switch it off anytime.
We never sell your data, show ads, or train AI models on your family's stories.
Delete everything?
Drawings, legends, stories, voices, recordings: all erased within 30 days. We'll email your stories first.
Built to meet COPPA (US children's privacy law). Questions go straight to Jon.
