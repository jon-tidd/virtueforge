How you'll know it's growing
They say "not yet" instead of "I can't."
They try a different way before asking for help.
They tell you about something hard they stuck with.
What to say when you spot it: praise the effort and the new way they tried ("You tried it a different way!"), not how smart they are. Toddlers who hear that kind of praise are more likely to enjoy challenges years later (Gunderson et al., 2013).
