How it works

Five small rhythms. One big idea: character is built by habit.

Aristotle said we become brave by doing brave things. We make that easy to practice, one bedtime at a time.

Tonight
7 min
Read one chapter where your kids are the heroes.
It's ready before bedtime. A "last time…" recap, the story, one question to pause and ask, and a cliffhanger. The kids choose where the heroes go next.
Tomorrow
10 sec
One small mission per child.
Try the big slide. Share an idea at dinner. Tell the truth about a mistake. When you spot it, tap once, and that night the story tells it back as a legend.
This week
Sunday, 2 min
One virtue, pitched at every age.
Print the fridge card and the week's booklet. On Saturday, each child tries one real-world "trial" of the week's virtue.
This season
12 weeks
A whole adventure, a whole cardinal virtue.
Four seasons a year: Courage, Justice, Wisdom, Self-control. The story map fills in, the kids' legends pile up, and at the end you can hold it all in a hardcover.
With family
Anytime
Grandparents join from anywhere.
Send tonight's picture to Grandma. She can read along, record tomorrow's chapter in her own voice, or give the kids a year.
