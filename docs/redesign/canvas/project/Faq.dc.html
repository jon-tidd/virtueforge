Questions

The honest answers.

Is this made with AI?

Yes, and we'll always tell you where. AI drafts each season from our story bible (the world, its people, its rules) and draws your kids. Every season is reviewed by a person before any child hears it. Anything personal (a legend, a special story) is checked, then shown to you first, so you never read something you haven't seen.

How do you draw my child without a photo?

You tap through choices: skin, hair, eyes, glasses, freckles, a favorite outfit. We draw a storybook portrait from that and keep it consistent in every chapter. We never ask for, or store, a photo of your child.

What ages is it for?

3 to 9. Little ones get a picture-book layout with big pictures and a few lines. Older kids get chapter-book pages. Siblings share one story, and each gets their own mission at their level.

How long does it take each night?

About 7 minutes to read, with one question to talk about. You choose a shorter or longer setting once, and we remember it.

What if we miss a night?

Nothing breaks. A rest night shows a moon, no lantern goes out, and the story waits on the same page for you.

Is it religious?

It's rooted in four classical virtues that families of every background share. Your kids meet them as Brave, Kind & Fair, Wise and Steady. If you'd like, turn on faith-friendly touches: a weekly verse and Bible stories in the library.

Isn't this more screen time?

We designed around that. You hold the phone in dim nightlight mode, or print the week's booklet, or play the audio. Pediatricians suggest no screens in the hour before bed. Paper and audio nights still light a lantern.

Can Grandma read in her own voice?

Yes. She can record a chapter on her phone. With Heirloom, she says a consent line aloud (kept, dated), reads for about 3 minutes, then previews her story voice, labeled “Made from Grandma’s recording, with her permission.” Grown-ups only, own voice only. It reads only our chapters, can’t be downloaded, and she can switch it off or delete it all.

What do you do with our information?

We keep first names, drawing choices, your stories, and any story voice with its consent record. No photos, no ads, no selling data. Download stories or delete everything anytime.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two taps. Your kids' legends and chapters stay downloadable.
