CHAPTER 28 · SOMETHING CRYING IN THE FOG

The sniffle came again, from somewhere past the next stone. Ember's ears went flat. "I don't like fog," she said. "I don't like wet paws. And I don't like sniffles I can't see."

This part was Clara's idea.

Hugh's knees wanted to shake. He noticed it, the way you notice rain. Then he did the thing Grandma Ruth had taught him: one slow breath in through his nose, and out so gently that a flame would only lean.

"Alfie, keep counting the stones," he said. "Clara, hold my cloak. We go together."

"Forty-one," said Alfie, very seriously. And there, curled on the next stone with its tail wrapped round its toes, was the smallest dragon any of them had ever seen.
