Why we built this

A dad of three who wanted bedtime back.

JT
Jon Tidd
Founder · dad of three · Burke, Virginia

I watched my kids get swallowed by screens, and I realized the stories that shaped my own character were disappearing from their childhood. The tales of courage, honesty and perseverance were being replaced by content built to keep them scrolling, not growing.

So I started making stories where my three were the heroes: brave when it was hard, kind when it was easy. They asked for "one more chapter" every night. That's Grit & Grace.

How every chapter is made

1
Drafted from our story bible. Human heroes, virtue rewarded, one question to ask, one thing to try tomorrow. AI helps us draft.
2
Read by a parent editor. Every season chapter is read by a person before any child hears it.
3
Illustrated with your kids. Drawn from your choices, checked for a consistent likeness.
4
Anything personal, you see first. Legends and special stories wait for your OK.

Why "Grit & Grace"

Grit is the courage to do hard things. Grace is the kindness to do them well. We want both for every child, boy or girl. The ancients called it virtue; we call it bedtime.
