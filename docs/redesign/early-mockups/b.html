The Lantern That Would Not Lie
❦

In the hill-town of Brindlewick, every child was given a lantern on her eighth birthday, and Maya's was the brightest of all.

But the lantern had a secret. Whenever its keeper spoke something untrue, its flame shrank to the size of a seed.

The first time it happened, Maya hardly noticed. "I didn't take the last honey cake," she said, crumbs still on her sleeve…
