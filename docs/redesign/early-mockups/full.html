HOMEPAGE 4/5 · WHY IT WORKS + OUR PROMISES

Why it works

Built on how children actually learn.

🪞
Seeing themselves changes behavior

Kids who heard a sharing story featuring themselves shared more than kids who heard the same story without it.

Early Childhood Education Journal, 2025 · 315 children
🌟
Goodness rewarded, not vice punished

Stories where honesty turned out well made kids more truthful. Pinocchio didn't.

Psychological Science, 2014 · replicated 2025
💬
"Why do you think…?" beats a lecture

Kids who explain the lesson apply it to new situations better than kids who are told the moral.

Cognition, 2017
📖
Stories that continue keep kids coming back

Serialized stories held children's engagement over time; stand-alone episodes lost it.

Communication Research, 2025
Our promises

What we'll never do.

✍️
Stories written by people

Every chapter is written and reviewed by a person. Illustrations are generated only to draw your kids in, and we check every one.

🔒
Never a photo of your child

You describe them; we draw them. Nothing we store can identify your kids. No ads, no data sales.

🌙
Screens off by lights-out

Read it from a printed booklet, press play on audio, or use nightlight mode. Pediatricians advise no screens an hour before bed, and we built around that.

🕊️
Five nights a week is plenty

No guilt-trip streaks. Rest nights are built in.
