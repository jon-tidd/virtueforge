THE PRODUCT, ON ONE PAGE

Five loops that turn a story into a habit.

Each loop has its own job. The nightly loop is the product; the others feed it and keep families coming back.

1 · Discover · once

From stranger to "that's me!" in 2 minutes
Homepage→Draw your child→Meet your heroes→Chapter 1 free→Save family→Set bedtime
Why: most trial cancellations happen on day one, so the wow comes before any signup. Likeness is what changes behavior.

2 · Nightly · 7:30pm, 5 of 7 nights

One tap, one chapter, one question
Reminder→Tonight→"Last time…"→Read→Pause & ask→Missions + cliffhanger→Lamp lit
Why: serialized stories hold engagement, and asking "why?" beats a lecture. The routine pays off at 5+ nights a week, and the screen stays dim.

3 · Daily · 10 seconds, optional

You notice it. The story tells it back.
"Did you spot it?"→Log a deed→Approve legend→Opens tonight's chapter
Why: recognition, not prizes; material rewards backfire. The lesson moves from the page into real life.

4 · Weekly · Sunday

One virtue, every child at their level
Recap email→Next virtue + "I can…" per child→Print fridge card & booklet→Saturday trial
Why: fewer concepts repeated beats a long list (your poster research), and paper keeps it off-screen.

5 · Seasonal & family · occasional

Why families renew and grandparents buy
Share card→Grandma reads / records→Gift a year·Season finale→Hall of Deeds→Hardcover→Renew
Why: only ~24% of education apps' annual plans renew, so the saga and the book give a reason to. Grandparents spend ~$2,654/yr on grandkids.
💳 Paywall: after Chapter 3 (night 3). By then they've seen their kids drawn in, felt a cliffhanger, and maybe had a deed told back. Family is $49/yr, all siblings included.
