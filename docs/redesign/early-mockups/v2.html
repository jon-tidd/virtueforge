REVISED · B · THE FRIDGE CARD (PRINTS SUNDAY)

The story leaves the screen. The virtue goes on the fridge, where the day actually happens.

One printed page a week: the virtue, each child's "I can…", and boxes to check when you spot it. It's your poster system, made weekly and automatic. There's no app to open during the day, and the fridge reminds everyone.

Why: seeing the routine on paper takes the load off memory (Harvard's executive-function research; the "First-Then" boards in your Drive doc). It also collects no child data, which helps with COPPA.
