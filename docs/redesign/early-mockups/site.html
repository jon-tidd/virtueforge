Why it works

Built on how children actually learn.

🪞
Seeing themselves changes behavior

Kids who heard a sharing story with their own face and name shared more than kids who heard the same story without it.

Early Childhood Education Journal, 2025 (315 children)
🌟
Goodness rewarded, not vice punished

Stories where honesty turns out well made kids more truthful. Pinocchio didn't.

Psychological Science, 2014; replicated 2025
💬
"Why do you think…?" beats a lecture

Asking kids to explain the lesson helps them apply it to new situations better than telling them the moral.

Cognition, 2017
🌙
Five nights a week is enough

The benefits of a bedtime routine grow with each night, so missing one isn't failure.

Sleep, 2015
