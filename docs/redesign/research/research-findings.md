Method note: ~30 targeted web searches. ## Scorecard

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skip/defer drawn likenesses | **Refuted** | 2025 RCT, 315 kids aged 5–6: a story with the child's photo + name raised sharing significantly more than the same story non-personalized. 2021 study: *name-only* personalization produced no gain. | Medium (one RCT, one behavior measure) |
| 2 | ~$79/yr price | **Refuted for a new brand** | RevenueCat 2025: Education median annual plan $43.94, monthly $8.13. Comps: Oscar Stories $49/yr; Epic & Moshi $79.99/yr (large libraries); Bedtimestory.ai ≈ $99/yr; PerfectTales $14.99/mo. | Medium-high |
| 3 | Hard paywall | **Mixed** | Hard paywalls convert ~5x (10.7% vs 2.1%) but one-year retention is similar; freemium wins when free users drive word of mouth. Education annual renewal only ~24%. 55% of 3-day-trial cancels happen Day 0 → the "wow" must land in the first session. | Medium-high |
| 4 | Podcast as the funnel | **Unverified** | Free podcast + paid club model exists (Story Pirates Creator Club: ad-free, bonus episodes, events). No conversion data found. | Low |
| 5 | AI-written stories as the core | **Weak** | NC State 2025 (qualitative, 13 families, kids 4–8): most parents accept AI *images* if text is human-authored and reviewed; most are *not* comfortable with AI-written text. Google Gemini Storybook (Aug 2025) makes free AI stories with photo-inspired characters; SCBWI/authors publicly critical. | Medium (small qualitative) |
| 6 | Curated / human-reviewed text | **Supported** | Same NC State finding; brand-risk logic. | Medium |
| 7 | Human-hero stories | **Supported (new)** | Larsen, Lee & Ganea 2018: human-character story raised preschoolers' sharing; the same story with animals *decreased* it. | Medium-high |
| 8 | Reward virtue, don't punish vice | **Supported** | Lee et al. 2014 (n=268, ages 3–7): George Washington story raised truth-telling; Pinocchio / Boy Who Cried Wolf did not. | Medium-high |
| 9 | Identity labels ("be a helper") | **Downgraded** | Bryan et al. 2014 original effect; later analysis g=0.12 with low replicability. | Low |
| 10 | Pause-and-ask (dialogic) prompts | **Supported** | Meta-analyses: moderate effects on vocabulary (d≈0.59 expressive; long-term programs g≈0.44); weaker for 4–5 yo. | High |
| 11 | Rewards/gamification | **Supported with limits** | Material rewards for helping reduced later helping in toddlers (Warneken & Tomasello 2008); praise did not. K-12 gamification meta g≈0.65 on motivation, high heterogeneity. | Medium |
| 12 | Screen-light bedtime (print/audio) | **Supported** | Bedtime screens → OR 1.72 for delayed bedtime; 2024 RCT removing toddler pre-bed screens improved sleep. Personalized books boosted vocabulary in print more than digital (Kucirkova). | Medium-high |
| 13 | Parent/grandparent voice | **Supported** | Abrams et al. 2016 (PNAS, n=24): mother's voice engages reward/emotion/social circuits far more than unfamiliar voices. | Medium |
| 14 | Consistency > perfection (rest nights) | **Supported** | Mindell 2015: bedtime-routine benefits are dose-dependent; 5+ nights/week is the common threshold. Lally 2010: habit automaticity 18–254 days (median 66). | High |
| 15 | COPPA "manageable" | **Mixed** | 2025 amendments add facial templates/biometrics; compliance deadline Apr 22, 2026. Child photos are personal information. BIPA suits hit AI-avatar apps (Lensa). Avatar-builder likeness (Hooray Heroes style) avoids storing faces. | Medium (get counsel) |

## Unit economics of likeness (rough, Sept 2026 list prices)
- Consistent-character image: ~$0.04 (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image $0.039; FLUX Kontext ~$0.04).
- AI narration: ElevenLabs $0.05–0.10 per 1K chars → ~$0.35–0.70 per 7-min story.
- Per family, 30 nights/mo: 3 new images/night ≈ $3.60; + AI narration ≈ +$10–21.
- At $49/yr (~$3.80/mo net), fresh images every night eat the margin. Fix: **1 personalized "hero moment" image per chapter + shared scene art** (~$1.20/mo), parent reads aloud by default, narration as premium/add-on. Printed books carry full personalization at $35–50 each (Wonderbly: 11M+ books sold; acquired by Penguin Random House, Jun 2025).

## Other data points
- Scholastic: 55% of kids 0–5 are read to 5+ days/week; 37% daily. (My earlier "64%→41%" stat is unverified — don't use.)
